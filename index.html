01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

A practical approach, with a personal touch.

There's something satisfying about a properly cleaned room, freshly made beds and knowing the everyday jobs are taken care of. I enjoy practical work and taking responsibility for keeping a home comfortable and organised.

My background includes several years of hands-on hotel housekeeping and working as Residence Manager at an official diplomatic residence in Budapest. My responsibilities covered everyday household care, supplies, maintenance arrangements and preparing for guests.

I work well independently and value clear communication, agreed routines and a friendly, respectful working relationship.

02 / PRACTICAL SUPPORT

How I can help

Every home is different. Some households need regular, detailed housekeeping; others appreciate additional help with laundry, family routines or organisation. The support I provide can be shaped around the home's needs and agreed routines.

Cleaning & Housekeeping

Regular and detailed cleaning, comfortable living spaces and keeping the home looking its best.

Laundry & Linen

Everyday washing, ironing, changing beds, linen care and wardrobe organisation.

Household Organisation

Keeping cupboards, storage areas and household essentials organised.

Family & Household Support

Children's laundry, room organisation and practical help with family routines.

Guests & Family Occasions

Guest-room preparation, table setting and help with family gatherings or special occasions.

Simple Home Cooking

Simple, fresh home-cooked meals as part of an agreed household routine.

Pets & Home Care

Pet routines and agreed care of the home while owners are away.

Household Coordination

Household supplies, deliveries and coordinating contractors or maintenance visits.

Responsibilities are always discussed in the context of each household and the time available.
